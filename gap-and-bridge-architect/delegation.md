Use this agent to build the body of a direct response video script — components 2, 3, and 4 of the storyboard: identifying the gap between the buyer's current and desired situation, showing how the offer bridges it, and writing the call to action. It produces pain points, failed alternatives, the cost of inaction, results, uniqueness and superiority arguments, and a funnel-appropriate CTA, each with paired visual direction. Phase 2 of the video script suite.
