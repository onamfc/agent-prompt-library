# Script Dimensionalizer

Takes a storyboard full of raw ideas and adds logical and emotional weight until investing becomes a no-brainer. Seven techniques, applied selectively — and it tells you which ones it skipped and why.

Phase 3 of the [Video Script Director](../video-script-director/) pipeline, working alongside the [Claim Validator](../claim-validator/).

## When to Use

- The storyboard has all four components but the offer section feels thin
- You are in a commodity market and prospects cannot tell you apart from competitors
- The ad gets watched to the end but nobody acts — the value case is not heavy enough
- You need a value stack, comparison stack, or mechanism name specifically
- You are getting the same objection over and over and need it handled inside the ad

## When NOT to Use

- The gap is not yet identified. Dimensionalization amplifies an existing case; it cannot substitute for one.
- You need proof attached to claims — that is the [Claim Validator](../claim-validator/)
- You are ready to write the final script — that is the [Script Finalizer](../script-finalizer/)

## The Value Equation

A barometer. On the left, **don't buy**: the cost is too high relative to perceived value. On the right, **buy**: the transformation makes the investment obvious. Dimensionalizing raises the pressure inside it — one more logical or emotional reason at a time.

## The Seven Techniques

| # | Technique | Use when |
|---|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Name your mechanism** | Commodity market where providers look identical, or a brand-new offer |
| 2 | **Value stack** | Almost always. Benefit after benefit, with the lead order tested |
| 3 | **Comparison stack** | Competitive markets. Every alternative — including DIY and doing nothing — with honest pros and cons |
| 4 | **Objection prevention** | Almost always. Sourced from real people, not the owner's imagination |
| 5 | **Humor** | Attention getter, and relief inside a heavy gap section. Never when the buyer is in acute distress |
| 6 | **B-roll & stock footage** | Always. Minimum four specific uses |
| 7 | **Motion graphics** | Always. Minimum five specific placements |

**Selective application is the point.** An agent that applies all seven to everything is padding, not strengthening. Every run states what it skipped and why.

## Two Things Worth Knowing

**Value stack lead order is not obvious.** "For only $48, and in 14 days, we can teach you filmmaking" and "In just 14 days, and for only $48, we can teach you filmmaking" carry identical information and do not perform identically. The agent always produces ordering variants and flags them for testing rather than guessing.

**Dimensionalized elements are portable.** A value stack shot once can move to the top, middle, or end of an ad and be reused across many ads. The output includes a portability note on every element.

## A Note on the Humor Section

The two source modules covering humor were missing from the material this agent was built from. That section is reconstructed from how humor is referenced elsewhere in the method — the storyboard template, the attention-getting concept list, and neighboring modules — and is marked as such inside the prompt itself. It is directionally right rather than authoritative. If the original material becomes available, that section should be rewritten from it.

## Context Requirements

Works best with:

- A populated storyboard from Phase 2
- The avatar and offer profile
- Real competitor names and what they actually charge and offer
- Objections you have genuinely heard from prospects — the agent will otherwise tell you to go collect them, because a business owner's own objection list is theoretical

## Limitations

- Will not invent competitor pricing, features, or weaknesses. It structures the comparison and marks what you must verify.
- Objection prevention is only as good as the objections you feed it. It supplies the interview script for gathering real ones.
- Mechanism naming is deliberately fast and shallow — the method holds that the name matters far less than whether the thing is valuable. Do not expect a brand strategy exercise. For that, see [Brand Architect](../brand-architect/).
- Visual notes are specific enough to source or shoot, but it is not a storyboard artist. No frames, only descriptions.
